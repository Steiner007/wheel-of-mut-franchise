    Weekly rules

    Every week (each player)

    2× Weekly Spin before the game. In the playoffs, spin the Playoff Spin instead.

    1× Weekly Challenge before kickoff. Your opponent is the referee.

    Blowout Bonus: win by 21+ and spin it once.

    Once a season

    Season Challenge: spin at the start. Finish it during the regular season for a Jackpot.

    Spin power & bonus spins

    Every Weekly and Playoff spin gets a power rating from ⚡1 to ⚡10. Wild Card is ⚡10 (max).

    Position counts. If the reward makes you spin the Position Wheel, its power is multiplied by the position: QB ×1.4, WR ×1.2, HB ×1.15, TE ×1.05, CB ×1.05, S ×0.9, LB ×0.9, DL ×0.85, OL ×0.75, K ×0.3, Your Choice ×1.4.

    Traits count too. On a Trait Pick, the trait has to fit the position. Fastest at WR or CB is ×1.3; Heaviest at WR is ×0.7. Best Overall ×1.3 and Smartest ×1.2 are good everywhere; jersey numbers and ABC order are ×0.6.

    Everyone always gets 2 spins. Add up the power of those 2: 7 or less = 2 bonus spins, 8–9 = 1 bonus spin, 10+ = no bonus. Bonus spins don't earn more bonus spins. Example: Position Player at K (⚡2) + Position Player at WR (⚡7) = ⚡9, so you get a 3rd spin.

    The page tracks your turn and tells you when you've earned a bonus spin. Tap New turn when the next player goes.

    Loss bonus

    Each new Weekly turn starts by asking how your last game went:

    ✅ I won / first game: no bonus.
    ❌ I lost: +1 comeback spin.
    🔥 Losing streak (lost your last 2+ games in a row): +2 comeback spins and a free Silver Pack (keep the best player).

    Comeback spins are added on top of your 2 spins and any weak-spin bonus. They don't count toward the "first 2 spins" weak check. Playoff turns don't ask, since you only spin there after a win.

    Win or lose

    Win: keep this week's players for the rest of the season. Lose: release them, unless you earned Protection from your challenge.

    Challenge rewards

    🛡️ Protection: if you lose this game, you still keep every player you earned this week. If you win, it does nothing. No pack.

    📦 Bonus Pack: after the game, open 1 Silver Pack and keep the best player. He's yours for the rest of the season, win or lose. It does NOT protect this week's other players.

    ⭐ Both: you get Protection and the Bonus Pack.

    Fail the challenge and you get nothing. Protection and bonus-pack players never survive the end-of-season reset.

    Trait Picks

    Spin the Position Wheel, then the Trait Wheel. The page shows the top 3 players in the league at that position for the trait, skipping anyone over 88 OVR. Take #1 (ties: you pick). Clone him if he's on a CPU team.

    The list comes from the Madden 26 roster file built into this page. If your franchise has a different roster (trades, signings, later seasons), double-check in Madden.

    Jackpots

    Pick any player in the league at that OVR or lower, any position. Clone him if he's on a CPU team.

    Position upgrades

    Swap your starter at a position for a better player at the same position. Example: you start an 80 OVR TE and get a +2, so you can take any TE rated 82 or lower. The old starter stays as a backup. If you upgrade over a prestige player or your rookie, the new player is not permanent.

    "Worst starter"

    Your lowest-rated starter on the depth chart (QB, HB, WR1–3, TE, OL ×5, DL ×4, LB ×3, CB ×3, S ×2). Ties: you pick.

    Packs

    Open packs in the Packs tab at the top of this page. The slice tells you which pack: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Elite, Team or Position. Bronze always comes as 5 packs (use the Open 5 button). Find the player in franchise and use his real franchise rating. If he's on a CPU team, clone him instead of taking him.

    Super Bowl

    Win it and spin the Super Bowl Bonus wheel once.

    Franchise setup guide
    1. Create the league. Online Franchise, you as commissioner, invite your opponent. Turn on Fantasy Draft. Enable player editing, force trades and roster edits.
    2. Spin the Team Wheel (each player).
    3. Difficulty: Simulation, All-Madden. Slider starting point: CPU QB accuracy / pass blocking / coverage / INTs about +10, same user sliders about −10. Adjust after week 1.
    4. Prestige players: each of you picks 1 offensive and 1 defensive player, 80 OVR or lower. Force-trade them to your team.
    5. Fantasy draft: every pick 69 OVR or lower (honor rule). CPU teams draft normally.
    6. Rookie: each of you takes one first-round rookie and keeps him permanently. He improves through Madden's normal progression.
    7. Spin Season Length at the start of each season.
    8. Play the weekly loop. Only games you actually play count for win/lose.
    9. Season end: release all earned players. Keep prestige players, your rookie, and anything the Super Bowl Bonus made permanent. Fill empty spots with free agents 69 OVR or lower.

    Menu names change by Madden year. Cloning = create a player on your team and edit him to match the original. If your version can't create players in franchise, force-trade the real one instead.
