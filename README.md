# Wheel of MUT Franchise

Spinner wheels and a pack opener for a 2-player Madden franchise played with Wheel of MUT-style rules.

Everything is in one file: open `index.html` in a browser. No build step, no server.

## What's in it

- **Wheels:** Weekly Spin, Playoff Spin, Position Wheel, Trait Wheel, Weekly Challenge, Season Challenge, Blowout Bonus, Super Bowl Bonus, Team Wheel, Season Length
- **Packs:** Bronze, Silver, Gold, Elite, Team and Position packs (5 cards each) with rip, flip and big-pull animations
- **Rules:** the weekly rules and franchise setup guide are in the dropdowns under the wheel

## Player data

Player names, teams, ratings, ages, speed and jersey numbers come from a Madden NFL 26 roster export (late-season ratings update, players rated 60+). Ratings are EA's. Use the rating in your own franchise file when the two disagree.

Fan-made. Not affiliated with the NFL or EA.
